Two instances walk the transfer: u_rd_agen over the source, u_wr_agen over the destination. Each hands dma_ctrl one burst at a time.

Inside dma_addr_gen

All datapath, except two control wires: load and burst_ack in, has_burst out. Click a block to see its code.

How many beats the transfer touches

A beat is 8 bytes on an 8-byte boundary. A transfer that starts 5 bytes into a beat touches those leading bytes' beat too, so the count is the offset plus the length, rounded up:

total_beats = (base[2:0] + len + 7) / 8

400 bytes from 0x0fa5: (5 + 400 + 7) / 8 = 51 beats. The same 400 bytes to 0x2002: (2 + 400 + 7) / 8 = 51.

Distance to the next 4 KB boundary

A burst may not cross a 4 KB page (the bus rule exists so a burst never spans two devices or two pages of a translation). In beats, the distance from addr_q to the next page is 512 minus the beat index within the page:

to_4k = 512 − addr_q[11:3]

At 0x0fa0: 512 − 500 = 12 beats.

The burst length: the smallest of three limits

MAX_BURST (16), the beats to the boundary, the beats left. Try a base just below a boundary, or a length that isn't a multiple of 128 bytes; hover a burst to highlight the line whose limit won.

400 bytes from 0x0fa5 read as bursts of 12 (cut at the 4 KB boundary at 0x1000), 16, 16 and 7 (the tail) beats:

12 · 4 KB16167
